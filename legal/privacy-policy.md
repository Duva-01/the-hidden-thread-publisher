# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-11

This policy describes the limited data used by the publishing tools operated for **The Hidden Thread**.

The tools may process account identifiers, OAuth tokens, video files, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and upload status information solely to authenticate the account and prepare or upload requested content.

Credentials are intended to be stored locally and are not intentionally sold or shared with third parties. Video content is sent only to the platform selected by the account owner for the requested upload. Local project files may be deleted after publication according to the channel workflow.

The tools do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unrelated to publishing. Third-party platforms may independently collect and process data under their own privacy policies.

To request clarification or deletion of locally stored publishing data, use the contact method associated with the **The Hidden Thread** channel.
